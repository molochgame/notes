## 130 Million Gallons

There are many reasons to be concerned about AI, and as a teenager I witnessed an injustice done to a friend, it influences me to this day as a father, it highlights the concerns I have around AI, and I swear this really happened.

In 2008, a few friends and I all bought the long awaited Call of Duty 4 map pack so we could play it together over the weekend. Internet speeds were bad, so this process took hours for some of us. We left our Xboxes on overnight.

Come Saturday, one friend joins voice chat but says he has to download it still. Why hadn't it downloaded overnight? Well, his dad had not let him leave his Xbox on; it would waste electricity. This wasn't an environmentally conscious man, he just didn't want to pay for it.

An idling Xbox consumes about 100 watts. If half the time overnight (4 hours) got wasted because the download finished, that would be 400 watt hours wasted. That does sound concerning... until you realize electricity prices were around [10 cents](https://www.eia.gov/totalenergy/data/annual/showtext.php?t=ptb0810) per 1,000 watt-hours. It would have cost him 4 pennies.
